Estimate your out-of-pocket costs under CHAMPVA
How CHAMPVA cost-sharing works: After your annual deductible ($50 individual / $100 family), CHAMPVA pays 75% of covered charges. You pay the remaining 25%, up to a $3,000 annual catastrophic cap.
