About CHAMPVA Finder

Why this exists, and how it works

Why This Site Exists

CHAMPVA doesn't require providers to enroll the way TRICARE or Medicare does — any willing provider can bill it. That's good news in theory, but in practice it means there's no official directory of who actually accepts CHAMPVA patients. Veteran families are left cold-calling offices one by one, often getting turned away by front-desk staff who've never heard of the program.

CHAMPVA Finder exists to fix that — a free, public directory built from federal provider data and, more importantly, from the real experience of families who've already found providers that work with CHAMPVA smoothly.

How the Directory Is Built

Most listings on this site started as records pulled from the federal NPI Registry (the same public database that identifies licensed healthcare providers nationwide). That gives good coverage, but it doesn't tell you whether a provider is actually easy to work with for CHAMPVA billing — only that they exist.

That's where the community comes in. Any visitor can add a provider they've personally used, and anyone can confirm or report a listing based on their own experience. Enough confirmations from real families upgrade a listing's badge from "unverified" to community-confirmed. It's not a perfect system, but it gets more accurate the more people use it.

A word of caution: Submissions go live immediately with no manual review queue, so treat this directory as a helpful starting point — not a guarantee. Always call ahead to confirm a provider is currently accepting CHAMPVA before booking an appointment.

Who's Behind This

CHAMPVA Finder is an independent, volunteer-run project — not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or any government agency. It was built because a directory like this should already exist and didn't. It's free to use and always will be.

Questions, Corrections, or Feedback?

Found a provider who should be listed, or something that needs fixing? Submitting is the fastest way to help other families right now.
